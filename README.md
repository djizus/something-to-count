# Something to Count

A novel, entered for the [Prix Thélyson-Orélien des nouvelles écritures](https://thelyson.ai/).

Maylis Lesca is the outdoor worker of a village on the Adour. She keeps the key to the floodgate. One night something sits on her chest, and she wakes with a picture in her head: a stone in the square with her own name on it and a date eighteen days away.

## Read it

- `book/something-to-count.pdf` is the reading copy.
- `manuscript/` holds the text, one file per chapter. `00.md` is the opening.

## Build it

```
python3 build.py "Something to Count" "Vera Shandles"
```

writes `book/something-to-count.md`, `.html` and, where LibreOffice is installed, `.pdf`.

## What else is here

- `PLAN.md`: the record of the project, round by round, with what each pass changed and what it measured.
- `bible.md`, `outline.md`: the world and the plot as first planned. The manuscript has moved on from both.
- `state.md`: who knows what, the facts the text has fixed, a calendar for each village, and what is still owed at the end.
- `briefs/`: the instructions given to writers, readers and critics for each pass.
- `audit/`: what the readers and critics reported.
- `drafts/`, `trial/`, `revision*.md`, `middle.md`: earlier working material.
