## Not settled

- **The book grew by 273 words (0.6%).** Chapters 1, 7 and 17 sit just under the ten per cent limit. I found no further cuts that the method justified.
- **The €3,400 is planted and not paid.** It works inside chapter 7 and nothing in 21 to 25 uses it. `state.md` lists it as owed nowhere. If that bothers the reviewer, the cleaner choice is to cut it from the book and the bible.
- **Sarthou's morning scene in 17 is gone.** Fitting the café inside the limit meant folding it in. His step and the "leave that house alone" hint survive, and the spirit-level exchange does not.
- **The box joke now appears only in 1 and 11.** Chapter 25's replacement has less to lean on.
- **One name in the dark drafts is not planted.** `drafts/21-dark.md` has a Nadine on the night shift. I added only the names the brief listed.
