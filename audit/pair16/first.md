# Pair 16: first

Thirteen of the seventeen chapters are word for word the same in A and B (0 to 6, 9, 11, 13 to 16). The verdict rests on chapters 7, 8, 10 and one sentence of 12.

## 1. Which one

A, slightly.

## 2. By criterion

- **Stakes: A.** Ch. 8, the squeeze test: "If she stays the whole of Friday night, there may be no hand to come back to." B puts no price on Maylis's own body.
- **Tension: A.** Ch. 7, straight after the corn buys Lesca the night: "From the chair she couldn't hear the breath any more, so she got up." The joke becomes a clock.
- **Progression: level.** Same events, same order. B's extra line, "The vote had left the gate shut for Friday afternoon," tells me what I had just watched.
- **Comedy: A.** "I've had the sandwich. I've told you those curtains want washing, twice, and you let me." And "She's the colour of this tape." I smiled at both. B has no joke A lacks.
- **Characters: A.** Arlette: "You never made a sound, even as a baby. I used to get up to see." Darrigrand: "You never had to."
- **The ending: level.** Identical.
- **Clarity: B, slightly.** A made me check twice: whose bed, at "In Lesca's own bedroom, under a dry roof, her mother had sat beside the bed", and at the squeeze test, why the owner's hand weakens when ch. 6 taught me the visitor is the one who weakens. In both versions I read Lesca's "One night matters" speech (ch. 8) twice.

## 3. The first fifty pages

No difference: the text is the same to the end of ch. 6. Hooked at the last line of ch. 2, "'Come back across,' her mouth said" (about page 15). Unable to stop from ch. 3, "That village hadn't had its flood yet" (about page 22).

## 4. The night of the flood

Maylis's choice is the same text in both, and I believed it. "She wanted it not to be her" is sixteen years of Either turned into an act, and "No job on that bank had ever taken her the time she gave it" had warned me her sum was wrong.

What differs is the fight for the handbrake just before. A: "It was the weak one, and it held." B: "It was one hand against the other, both of the same strength, and the one on the brake had got there first." A's hand wins by will, and it is the hand I watched fail on Sunday. B's reads like a referee's ruling. Lesca letting go also weighs more in A, because I had seen her body going cold. A.

## 5. The last two chapters

Level. They are the same, and they end well: letter No. 42 and the second shovel.

## 6. What B does better

Not much. Most of B's changes are cuts.

- Ch. 8: "On Saturday the water had been over his ankles. It had come up a step a day since." A gives the rate without the starting point.
- Ch. 7: "Maylis read it with her." It reminds me she is watching the post, before she uses that reading on Sunday.
- Ch. 7, the join B gets by cutting: "Then he looked down his track to where it ended, and drove on." / "'Thirty-four. Three, four.'" A puts the afternoon bedside scene between them. The midnight one does the same job harder, and A could keep only that one.

Not worth taking: B's second look at the wardrobe in ch. 10 repeats a beat from the same scene.

## 7. Against the comparison

Level with The Good Place, below Crouch; slightly below overall. The comedy and the moral trap hold up: a trolley problem with a floodgate, and an afterlife that is bingo night, where the mayor sends her apologies. What decides it: from ch. 7 to ch. 10 the danger is a date on a calendar or a body in the other village. Crouch would put it in the room.

## 8. Afraid for anybody, chapters 5 to 10

- **5 (same in both).** Yes, twice. For Jeannot: "I hadn't given the 11th a thought until you handed it to me." The warning is what sends him out. And on the stairs, for Maylis and Coralie, that Lesca would get into that bed.
- **6 (same).** Yes, the most in this stretch. For Maylis: the pulse going from 64 to 36, Coralie's thumb over the doctor's number, the thing five hours late.
- **7.** A: yes, for Lesca, that she stops breathing in her own bed while the hall plays for a duck. B: nobody's life. Only that Coralie takes the fourth step to the wrong woman, which A has too.
- **8.** Both: for the forty-two beds once the vote goes four to three, and at the card with the name on it. A adds fear for Maylis herself, that she gets less of her body back each time.
- **9 (same).** Not during the 2014 night; I knew he drowned. Afraid for nine rings that Jeannot would not answer.
- **10.** B: no. Sad for both women, not afraid. A: a little, for Lesca, grey on the stage with her hand on the stone, about to cross again.
