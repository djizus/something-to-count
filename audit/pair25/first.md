## 2. By criterion

- **Stakes: B.** Ch. 3, the typed file line: "12 December 2014. Body of CASTAGNET Jean recovered at first light". It explains the date on the stone. A's "First light." does not.
- **Tension: level.** Ch. 14: A's "She had got Lesca out of her, but too late" reads at speed; B's "it had come too late" stopped me.
- **Progression: level.** Same scenes, same order.
- **Comedy: level.** I smiled in the same places. B's Kévin and Jeannot are saltier; A's "'Against,' the deputy mayor said" (ch. 16) is what lets "I abstain" land.

- **The ending: A, slightly.** See 5.
- **Clarity: A, clearly.**
