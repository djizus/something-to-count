## Chapter 12 (2)

**12.1 (4, reads as a mistake: a time that can be taken for another; line 139).**
"'You rang me at 5,' he said."
Three paragraphs up the telling says "By 5 Maylis was up on the bank", which is 5 in the afternoon. Jeannot means her call at 5 that morning (chapter 10). With the two 5s so close, the line reads as a call she has just made from the bank.
Correction: "'You rang me at 5 this morning,' he said."

**12.2 (4, reads as a word missing; a phrase left behind; line 147).**
"'Then I'm dry.' He pointed his chin at the fields. 'The river over the line on that side, and a hand of water on my side. That's what a bank is for.'"
"A hand of water" reads as a word missing. Chapter 1 now has "a hand's width of water", and the other places in this chapter that used "a hand" for a depth have been reworded. This is the one that was left.
Correction: "'Then I'm dry.' He pointed his chin at the fields. 'The river over the line on that side, and a hand's width of water on my side. That's what a bank is for.'"

## Chapter 14 (2)

**14.1 (5, pronoun; a new sentence took the owner away; line 33).**
"She had built that patch herself in April, one wheelbarrow at a time."
The paragraph before it is now "Lesca was gone. The water kept coming." So the last woman named is Lesca, who repaired this same bank every spring in her own village. "She" is Maylis. Before the last changes the sentence above it had Maylis as its subject.
Correction: "Maylis had built that patch herself in April, one wheelbarrow at a time."

**14.2 (6, inconsistency with chapter 12: left and right; line 137).**
"The river was on her right, close and loud. On her left, down below, there were no street lamps any more."
Here she is running from the gate towards the Port. In chapter 12 (line 295) she sits in the van facing the gate, with the Port behind her, and there too the river is on her right: "On her right the river went by, close under the top of the bank, with a sound like a crowd far away. On her left Jeannot was sitting in his cellar. Behind her, under the bank, were Sarthou…" She has turned around between the two, so the river cannot be on her right both times. One of the two sentences has to change. Chapter 14 is the smaller change.
Correction: "The river was on her left, close and loud. On her right, down below, there were no street lamps any more."

## Chapter 17 (1)

**17.1 (5, pronoun; a corrected sentence; line 181).**
"'Tomorrow at 10,' he said. 'Madame Etcheto's daughter wants the words she said each night to be said over her. I've had them written out for me, and I'm not sure they're a prayer.'"
The sentence used to begin "Her daughter wants". Now that the daughter is the subject, "she" and "her" attach to her: the daughter's own words, said over the daughter.
Correction: "'Tomorrow at 10,' he said. 'Madame Etcheto's daughter wants the words her mother said each night to be said over her. I've had them written out for me, and I'm not sure they're a prayer.'"
