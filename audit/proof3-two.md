## 06 (2)

**6.1** Line 135. Kind 4, a verb in a rare sense. New sentence.
> That left Maylis alone in the town hall until 2, and the file was what she had crossed for.

"Cross" with no object, for going from one village to the other, was last used in chapter 3 ("So she could cross"). In this chapter Maylis has just walked "across the square", so the sentence reads as crossing the square to get to a file. The book's other verb for it is "come over" (04, "this is what I came over for"; 09, "When she comes over into me").

Corrected: That left Maylis alone in the town hall until 2, and the file was what she had come over for.

**6.2** Line 165. Kind 5 (Inguma).
> Then she told it to leave the roadsides and come for her now.

The sentence before it ends on another noun: "Maylis said the words to the ceiling." For half the sentence "it" is the ceiling. With the name in, the next line ("It didn't come.") follows a name too.

Corrected: Then she told Inguma to leave the roadsides and come for her now.
