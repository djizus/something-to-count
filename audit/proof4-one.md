## 03 (2)

**3.1** Line 7. Kind 6, "ago" handled two ways.
> A minute ago she had been asleep in a steel chair, with somebody warm sitting in it beside her.

The last revision changed the other two "ago" in the narration to "earlier": "That was twelve years ago, in June 2014" became "Twelve years earlier, in June 2014" (01), and "a February eleven years ago" became "a February eleven years earlier" (03, line 213). This is now the only "ago" in the narration of the whole book, and it sits with "had been", the same case as the two that were changed.

Corrected: A minute earlier she had been asleep in a steel chair, with somebody warm sitting in it beside her.

**3.2** Line 187. Kind 6, a month that does not agree with the date of the scene.
> Stars, in December, under cloud.

The scene is the evening of Saturday 28 November: "Today was the 28th" is forty lines above, and the day Lesca asks for in her next speech is Monday the 30th. The sentence is old, not one of the new ones. If Arlette means the winter in general, it can stay.

Corrected: Stars, in November, under cloud.
