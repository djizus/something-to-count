## 06 (1)

**6.1** Line 163. Kind 5. Left over from a cut.
> When the door had shut she got down on the mayor's floor, on her back, beside the filing cupboard, with her arms by her sides. Maylis said the words to the ceiling.

The sentence before this one is "'Give me ten minutes,' Maylis told the secretary", so two women are in the room when "she" arrives. Then the name comes one sentence after the pronoun, which reads as if somebody other than Maylis had got down on the floor. The sentence that used to stand between the two ("That gave Inguma somewhere to sit.") has gone, and it was what made the name natural. This is the slightest of the three. It is the same case as "She sat still", line 149, which was mended for the same secretary.

Corrected: When the door had shut Maylis got down on the mayor's floor, on her back, beside the filing cupboard, with her arms by her sides. She said the words to the ceiling.

## 07 (1)

**7.1** Line 105. Kind 5. Left over from a cut.
> Maylis stood on his trapdoor in the rain.

Jeannot was last named fourteen lines up (line 91), and the paragraphs in between are about Lesca, the watch, the bank and the gate.

Corrected: Maylis stood on Jeannot's trapdoor in the rain.
