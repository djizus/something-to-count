## 05 (1)

**5.1** Line 181. Kind 4, a word left over from the sentence that was changed.
> Lesca didn't know there was another half.

Two lines above, "Then she looked across the table for the other half of the laugh" became "Then she looked across the table for Maylis to laugh with her." That was the only place that said a laugh had halves. Without it, "another half" has nothing to be the half of, and a reader stops to ask: half of what? One cold reader thought this sentence would read at once after the change and the other asked for both to change; only the first change was made. Read as it now stands, it does not read at once. It is the one weak spot left in these chapters and it is small.

Corrected: Lesca didn't know she was meant to laugh too.

Or, keeping "half" and giving it its noun: Lesca didn't know that laugh had another half.
