## Chapter 12 (1)

**12.1 (4, a word order a reader stumbles on; the sentence is new; line 135).**
"It went past a metre under the boards of the platform."
The sentence used to be "It went past just under the boards of the platform." With "a metre" in the place of "just", the reader takes "went past a metre" together, as in "went past the house", and has to start again at "under". A comma keeps every word and removes the stumble.
Correction: "It went past, a metre under the boards of the platform."
