## 1

**1.1** — kind 5
> 'Don't. He'd only ring me to dig the hole.'

- Thought: a joke I was missing. Why would a priest ring her about a hole?
- Turned out: she digs the village's graves. The book says so a page later ("Maylis dug the graves in Peyrebarthe").
- Change: give her the job before the joke. Earlier in the scene, "She lifted sacks of cement for a living" becomes "She dug graves and lifted sacks of cement for a living".

**1.2** — kind 1, then 5
> What we want to know is whether, somewhere, you also see the other one.

- Thought: see the other lamp where? In her mind? In a mirror?
- Turned out: whether another Maylis, somewhere else, sees the other lamp. I had it only at Coralie's line, "to see whether there were two of you".
- Change: "What we want to know is whether there is another you, somewhere, who sees the other one."

**1.3** — kind 4
> Maylis was the village's only outdoor worker, so she spent it in the square, raking plane leaves off the drain covers.

- Thought: "plane leaves" — flat leaves? Something from an aeroplane?
- Turned out: leaves of the plane trees, named at the end of the chapter.
- Change: "raking the leaves of the plane trees off the drain covers".

**1.4** — kind 5
> 'You didn't have to be so quick,' Larrère said.

- Thought: quick at what? She had only stood there with a rake.
- Turned out: quick to sign for the letter he was refusing to sign for. Jeannot explains it a few lines on.
- Change: "'You didn't have to sign so quickly,' Larrère said."

**1.5** — kind 1 and 4
> He nodded towards the flood fields, where his duck hut was a cellar under a trapdoor in the grass.

- Thought: a hut where he keeps ducks, and somehow the hut is a cellar.
- Turned out: a hole in the ground where he sits at night to shoot wild ducks. The word "hunt" does not come until chapter 9.
- Change: "where he shot ducks from his hut, a cellar under a trapdoor in the grass".

**1.6** — kind 5
> Sixteen years, and I've had one straight answer out of you, and that was me.

- Thought: that Coralie had given the straight answer herself.
- Turned out: the one straight answer was the word "You", meaning Coralie. Clear after the flashback.
- Change: "and the answer was me."
