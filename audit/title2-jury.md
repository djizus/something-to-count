# Two titles

## 1. Cold, on a cover

- *Something to Count*: a quiet, slightly odd book, perhaps about sleeplessness or a small ritual; it makes me curious and promises nothing.
- *Count for Something*: an uplifting story about an overlooked person who wants to matter; I expect a lesson.

## 2. Once read

- *Something to Count*: the work you give the thing on your chest to keep it off, and then everything else that gets counted: days, turns, pulses, fingers, heads in a hall. Its one risk is sounding like a children's counting book. Idiomatic.
- *Count for Something*: the village's girl whose name is not even on her coat, wanting to matter, with a pun on the counting. The pun is good, but the phrase states the theme and reads as a moral, even as an order. Idiomatic, and a little worn.

## 3. Funny and then not, and gives least away

*Something to Count*. It is light before the book and heavy after it. *Count for Something* is earnest from the start and tells the reader what to feel.

## 4. Choice

***Something to Count.*** Sure: 4 out of 5.
