# Two titles

## Before reading

*Something to Count* suggests a quiet, possibly eccentric literary novel about someone occupied with small particulars; among forty prize entries, its modesty might intrigue or let it disappear.

*Count for Something* suggests a novel about making a life matter, with a motivational edge that would make it less distinctive in that list.

## After reading

*Something to Count* names the work assigned to Inguma, then acquires a darker second meaning through the counting of survivors and the place left empty; it is natural English.

*Count for Something* means that choices, testimony and unpaid work must matter, while its second meaning joins numerical counting to moral worth; it is idiomatic English, though the imperative sounds like an exhortation.

## Fit and choice

*Something to Count* better suits a book that is funny and then is not: the same ordinary activity passes from comic distraction to a count of forty-one. It gives less away about the moral reckoning and makes no promise of personal fulfilment.

My choice is *Something to Count*. Confidence: **4/5**.
