## Lines that fail

### 1. Ch 6: "It was easy at a funeral."

**Check 3.** The scene is a minute of silence, so "It was easy at a funeral" reads for a moment as if this were the funeral and the thing were easy now. The next sentence then says she cannot do it, with no word to mark the turn.

Mend: "At a funeral that was easy. Here all she could find was Jeannot and his letter seventeen, …"

### 2. Ch 7: "Ten was the time it took to hook the trailer up."

**Check 3.** "Ten" has no unit. The reader has to carry it back over "I had my hand on it" to Lesca's "ten minutes". It is also the second line of this kind in six lines, after "Twenty-five minutes was a trailer of leaves to the dump and back", which has its unit.

Mend: "Ten minutes was the time it took to hook the trailer up."

### 3. Ch 3: "The paper lampshade: hers. The top of the wardrobe: hers. The clock radio, 06:00 in red: hers. The rain: not hers."

**Check 3.** Two sentences earlier she is "in a bed that wasn't hers", where "hers" means what it says. In the list it means "the same as hers", and nothing in this room is hers. This is the paragraph where the reader first learns there are two rooms, so the word must not point both ways. "The rain: not hers" also asks rain to belong to someone.

Mend: "The paper lampshade: same. The top of the wardrobe: same. The clock radio, 06:00 in red: same. The rain: different."

### 4. Ch 12: "Hontaube had two."

**Check 3.** The sentence before it already says "its two outdoor workers". The reader is told the number twice in a row.

Mend: take "two" out of the sentence before: "She asked to borrow its outdoor workers for one night, at the Port. Hontaube had two. Peyrebarthe had her."

### 5. Ch 9: "Maylis would have sent him up in the tractor's bucket."

**Check 3.** In chapter 6 "Maylis would have taken the trailer" works because she is a visitor in the other village and the job is not hers. Here the village, the tractor and the lights are hers, and in chapter 2 she did send him up in the bucket. "Would have" makes the reader ask who stopped her. It is also the same remark about the same man three chapters later.

Mend: cut it. Kévin's "I was up the ladder on Tuesday" in chapter 12 still has the ladder sentence to stand on.
