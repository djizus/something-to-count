# Brief: one fight for the body comes out

Three literary critics said the same thing: the two women fight for one body too often in chapters 5 to 9 (the stair post, the bingo bar, the landing, the kitchen table), so that by the handbrake in chapter 13 the contest has become arm-wrestling. JC agrees. One bout goes: **the kitchen table in chapter 9.**

In `manuscript/09.md`, the Sunday breakfast scene. From "Lesca took a breath for the words." to "Under Coralie's hands the left hand went soft.", the right hand goes over the mouth, the left drags at its fingers, the nose is blocked, and Coralie pins the left wrist. Take that out.

What the scene still has to do, in this order and in few sentences:

- Something heavy is set down on the step outside the kitchen door. Lesca takes a breath for the words that would send it away to count.
- **Coralie stops her.** She has the chart in front of her, she asked "And what puts her out?", and she sat beside Madame Etcheto last night and watched the words being said. She reaches across the bread and puts her own hand flat over the mouth. One hand, no struggle between the two women inside the body. She can keep "I do this all week" if it still fits what her hand is doing; otherwise it goes.
- Lesca gets one word out around Coralie's fingers: "Friday."
- The door doesn't open. Inguma is on the chest all the same, at the kitchen table, with its leaves against the bread. Lesca is gone.
- Maylis has the whole of herself back, and her right hand is slow. Coralie holds out two fingers: "Squeeze." The lines that follow ("That's as hard as I can", the stair post, "I may not get even the hand back") stay as they are.

Cut "They had one nose between them, so now neither of them could breathe" and everything that belongs to the fight. Then read chapters 9 to 13 for any later line that refers back to the hand over the mouth or the nose at the kitchen table, and mend it in the fewest words.

Change nothing else in the chapter. The plain style of `briefs/line.md`; no "lay" or "lain" as the past of "lie"; Inguma is "it"; clock times in figures.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.

Reply in three lines: what the passage now does, any later line you mended, and the word count of chapter 9 before and after.
