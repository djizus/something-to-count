# Brief: one cold read

A short novel is in `scratch/calib2/A/`, in nineteen chapters, `01.md` to `19.md`. Read it in full, in order. Open nothing else but this brief.

You are a reader who likes Blake Crouch and The Good Place, and you are deciding whether to hand this book to a friend with the same taste. Judge only what is on the page. If you think you recognise the book or its author, do not say so, and do not let its reputation count for or against it.

Write `audit/calib2/<your name>.md`, under 700 words:

1. **Would you hand it to that friend**, and with what warning, if any.
2. **By criterion**, one line each with the passage that decided it: stakes, tension, progression, comedy (funny means a reader smiles), characters, the ending, clarity (list each page you had to read twice).
3. **Chapter by chapter**: were you afraid for anybody, and would you have stopped there.
4. **Against the comparison.** Is it as good as a Blake Crouch novel and as The Good Place for a reader who likes both? Below, level or above, and the one thing that decides it.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.

Reply in two lines: the verdict in section 4, and the one thing.
