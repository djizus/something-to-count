# Brief: apply the cold read

Three cold readers read the book after the last cuts: `audit/cold-reader.md`, `audit/cold-ab.md`, `audit/cold-continuity.md`. Apply what follows and nothing else. Find each sentence by its words.

Writer one has chapters 00 to 08. Writer two has chapters 09 to 17.

## Part 2 of each report (errors)

Apply every correction in part 2 of all three reports for your chapters. "baby carriage", "dish towel", "truck" and "the dump" stay.

## Part 1 (places a reader went back)

Mend a place only if **two or three of the readers quote the same sentence or the same passage**, and these, which are decided:

- **Chapter 3, first sentence.** All three took Lesca for Maylis. It becomes: "Lesca, the mayor, was lying on her back in a bed that wasn't hers, listening to the rain on the roof."
- **Chapter 7.** "On Wednesday and Thursday nights Maylis had pushed Inguma off until 4, and Lesca had stayed at home." becomes "On Wednesday and Thursday nights Maylis had pushed Inguma off until 4 and never reached the chair."
- **Chapter 9.** "'It came out in my kitchen.'" gets its speaker: "'It came out in my kitchen,' Maylis said."
- **Chapter 13**, the hall of the dead: already mended. Leave it.

A mend makes the line plainer and is as small as it can be. It must not state a rule of the dream in the narrator's voice, must not explain what a gesture or a line of speech meant, and must not tell the reader an outcome before the scene reaches it. If the only mend a report offers does one of those, leave the sentence as it is. Do not put back any sentence about words sticking in the steel room.

## Rules

- Change nothing else. The plain style of `briefs/line.md`. No "lay" or "lain" as the past of "lie". Inguma is "it".
- Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.

Reply in three lines: how many corrections you applied from part 2 and from part 1, any you left and why, and the word count.
