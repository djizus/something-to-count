# Brief: a cold read in two parts

Read `manuscript/00.md` to `17.md` in order, in full, at the speed you read a thriller. Open nothing else but this brief. You know nothing about how the book was made or what anyone has said about it.

You are a fluent reader whose first language is not English. You read Harlan Coben and Blake Crouch in English without ever going back up the page.

Write `audit/cold-<your name>.md` in two parts.

## 1. Every place you went back

Note every place where you:

1. read a sentence or a paragraph twice to understand it;
2. went back to an earlier page to check a fact, a name or a date;
3. did not know for a moment who was speaking, whose head you were in, which village you were in, or whose hand was doing what;
4. met a word you would have to look up;
5. could not tell **how or why something happened**: how one woman gets into the other's village, why one of them wakes in the other's bed, how long she can stay, why a word will not come out in the steel room, what the rain has to do with either of them, where a person ends up and why. The book shows these things happening and does not explain them. Say for each whether you understood it from what you were shown, understood it late, or never understood it.

For each: the chapter, the sentence quoted exactly as it stands, which of the five it was, and the smallest change that would have let you read it once. The change must make the line plainer. It must not tell the reader an outcome before the scene reaches it, and it must not be a rule stated by the narrator.

Give the total and the count for each chapter. Do not pad.

## 2. Errors

Every grammar error, typo, punctuation slip, pronoun with an unclear owner, or sentence that is correct but would make a fluent reader think it is a mistake. The creature Inguma is always "it". Clock times and dates are in figures. Spelling is British. For each: the chapter, the sentence quoted exactly, and the corrected sentence in full. Do not list deliberate fragments or deliberately broken speech.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git and do not edit the manuscript.

Reply in two lines: the total for part 1 and how many were of kind 5, naming any you never understood; and the count for part 2.
