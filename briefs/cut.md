# Brief: making the cut

Five rounds of critics gave the same sentence: "I would have to tell them to hold on until chapter 15." Four plans added to the first fourteen chapters and the sentence did not change. `revision5.md` cuts them instead: fourteen chapters become nine, the book goes from 57,500 words to about 44,000, and the night at the gate arrives three fifths of the way in. Read `revision5.md` in full, and section 4 of each `audit/round5-*.md` for what must not be touched.

The book as the critics read it is in `manuscript-v5/`. The plan names its scenes by present chapter and letter, in the order of the section breaks. You write the new chapters into `manuscript/NN.md`, numbered as the plan numbers them.

## For each chapter that is yours

1. Lay the kept scenes in the plan's order, each cut to the length it gives. Cut what the cut list names first. Restore the three things the plan lists under "What does not fit", so that the book reaches 44,000: the roof, the whole of the counter, and Marie-Jo's doorstep.
2. Make the joins the plan lists, in a few lines each. Write no new scene.
3. A kept scene is cut, not rewritten. Keep its best lines whole. Where a cut removes something a later line leans on, mend the later line.
4. The chapter ends on the line the plan gives, with nothing after it.
5. For the last eight chapters, which are the present 15 to 22 renumbered, copy the text and make only the edits the plan names.
6. Read each finished chapter once as a stranger would. Every sentence can be read once, the reader knows which woman it is and whose body, and each step back in time is marked by its tense.

## Rules

- Load the `stop-slop` skill and audit each chapter.
- British spelling. Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not edit the plan, the bible or `manuscript-v5/`, and do not run git. Leave `state.md` alone.
- Reply when all your chapters are written: one line a chapter with its word count and anything you decided that the plan did not.
