# Retrofit for chapters 1 to 15

Three changes land together: the figure becomes a creature, the village is placed on the Basque bank of the Adour with its names unchanged, and five things the cold reader could not follow are said plainly. The bible has the decisions, in sections 2, 5 and 11. This file says what changes in each merged chapter. Anything not listed stays as it is. Suggested wording is a guide and gets rewritten in the book's voice.

## 1. Names

Every person and place keeps the name it has in the chapters. The village sits on the Basque bank at the edge of the Landes, where names are as often Gascon as Basque, so nothing is renamed and no initial or capital changes. The one new name is a minor one, Madame Etcheto at the care home.

## 3. The figure, in one paragraph

It is one creature the size of a ewe, a hunched back of dry brown leaf over riveted plate, with a back on every side and no face or hands. At 02:51 it is on top of the wardrobe. It comes down, crosses the boards and gets onto the bed in one movement, and sits on her breastbone turned away from her. The bible gives its five rungs and what a writer may repeat. The words "weight", "thirty-five kilos", "cold steel" and "old tobacco" stop being the way it is announced.
