# Brief: the held question, settled

The cold reader's hole: why one village had the rain of 2014 and the other did not, and why the same rain reaches the other twelve years later, day for day.

JC asked that little change, so the answer adds no machinery and nobody states a cause. The text lets a pattern show and stops there.

- **Why it did not rain there in 2014** needs nothing. The weather of the two villages parted within weeks of the box, as the bible says. One clause where Lesca first learns it, saying that the two skies had not matched since that June, is enough.
- **Why it rains there now** is the pattern. It began to rain in Maylis's village on the morning of 24 November 2026, which is the morning after the first night both women lay under the creature at the same minute. Since then the river there has followed the readings of 2014 day for day. Lesca has both sets of figures in front of her in chapter 14. She notices the date the rain began, and she notices that it is the date of that first night. She does not say what it means and neither does the narrator.
- **What a reader may conclude** is that the night she has carried for twelve years is the largest thing to have crossed, and that the creature was holding it back. That is consistent with its ladder and with both women getting up from the chair. No character says it.
