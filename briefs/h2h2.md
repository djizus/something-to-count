# Brief: two openings

The opening pages of two novels are in `scratch/h2h2/X.md` and `scratch/h2h2/Y.md`, about 1,250 words each. They are by different writers and have nothing to do with each other. Read both, in the order you are given. Open nothing else but this brief.

You are a reader who likes fast, high-concept thrillers and character comedy. You have time for one of these two books. If you think you recognise either, do not say so, and do not let its reputation count for or against it: judge these pages only.

Write `audit/h2h2/<your name>.md`, under 400 words:

1. **Which one do you go on reading**, X or Y, and how sure: clearly, slightly, or cannot choose.
2. **By criterion**, X or Y or level, one line each with the words that decided it: the hook (a reason to turn the page), who you care about so far, the voice, clarity (did you read anything twice), the promise each makes about the book to come.
3. **The quality of the writing alone**, sentence by sentence: X better, Y better, or the same standard.
4. **What each should learn from the other**, one line each.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.

Reply in two lines: which, how sure, and why.
