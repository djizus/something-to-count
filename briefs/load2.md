# Brief: the load, second part

Every reader of round 24 put clarity at 5.5 to 6, and JC found the book tough to follow. The first part of this pass cut the rules down. This part cuts the other things a reader has to carry: names that look alike, and a legal route that leads nowhere. Read `audit/round24-reader.md` faults 1, 6, 7 and 9, `-thriller.md` fault 8 and `-characters.md` fault 6.

Most of this is deletion. Add only what is listed.

## Names

- **The dealer** is "the dealer" in every chapter. He is never Lassalle and never Fernand.
- **The secretary** of the town hall is "the secretary". The name Ducasse goes.
- **The deputy mayor** is "the deputy mayor". The name Tastet goes.
- **The priest** is "the priest". The name Honoré goes.
- The woman who ran the experiment is "the woman from the university". The name Kessler goes.
- Madame Dufau becomes **Madame Poey**. Monsieur Castéra becomes **Monsieur Hourcade**.
- Lafitte has already been renamed **Bordenave** in every chapter; leave it.
- Where a line names two people whose names begin with the same letter, say which is which by what he is doing.

## The legal route

A reader must be able to say in one line who can open the gate and why he will not: **Larrère, as president of the association that owns it, and nobody else; and he says shut.**

- **The prefect has no part in the gate.** Take out the letter to the prefect, its envelope, its answer and "Pau sent me this". In chapter 4, when Maylis asks whose order, Lesca says Larrère's, and that on Monday she will ask him, in office hours. Monday delivers him, at a car window. Cut the promise of a gendarme and the director of the care home, and the question at ten past six that asks for the director.
- The statutes in chapter 6 are two lines. The council keeps Larrère's speech with no bell and the question the hand cuts off. Maylis is not in that village to look anything up; cut the errand.
- The prefect stays where he is a man at a ceremony.
- **Hontaube does not take over the key in January.** Delete those lines. Keep "Hontaube opens that file and decides who it keeps", so that a clean file is a job and a dated letter is the end of one.
- In Larrère's folder there are two sheets and no more: her resignation without a date, and the sheet with Kévin's name.

## Kévin takes the alibi away himself

Before five o'clock on the Friday, Kévin tells her he would do the six months, and that she is not to keep a gate shut for him. A few lines, where he already is. Then "Either" on the platform has nothing to hide behind.

## The ground, once

In chapter 1, in the square or on her way to it, two or three sentences in Maylis's own words, as the person who works it: along the bank, in order, the care home, the patch, the gate, the track and the blind; the meadows are a basin made to take the river and give it back on the next tide; the Port has no such room. Then cut the same explanation wherever it comes later, except Lesca's one line on the platform.

## The first two chapters carry less

- Chapter 1: Jeannot's numbered quarrel about the gate comes after the reader knows what the gate is for, and is shorter.
- Chapter 2: cut the association's meeting on the bank; "The keyholder has no preference" goes with it. Thursday and Friday are one night. The other woman's sentence comes out of Maylis's mouth sooner for it.
