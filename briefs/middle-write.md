# Brief: writing the new middle

Eleven rounds of readers say the same thing: chapters 11 to 18 are level with Blake Crouch or above him, and chapters 6 to 10 are where every reader stops. Nine revisions patched those chapters. This time they are written fresh, in one hand: yours.

Read `middle.md` in full: it is the design, and it is to be followed. Then `manuscript/00.md` to `05.md` and `11.md` to `18.md` for the voice and for what you are joining, `bible.md` sections 5 and 10, and the three rules of the crossing. `manuscript-v10/06.md` to `10.md` hold the old middle: take from it only the lines the design tells you to keep.

Write `manuscript/06.md` to `manuscript/10.md`, replacing what is there. About 2,000 words each.

- **A reader of thrillers must be unable to stop.** In the first hundred words of each chapter the reader knows what she is trying to do. Someone with a face stops her. Something turns that the reader did not expect. The last line is a danger that has just got nearer, and nothing follows it.
- **Play every scene.** Nothing is reported that could be shown, and nothing is told twice.
- **When two share a body, name who acts in every sentence that has an act in it.** Maylis shut the hand. Lesca opened it. A reader must never stop to work it out.
- **The narrator is the book's own**: third person, past tense, dry, exact, British spelling. He never winks and never explains. Use the pluperfect whenever the story steps back.
- **The comedy is situation**: a woman in the wrong village with the wrong habits, in front of people. It leaves the reader more worried than it found them.
- **Say plain things plainly.** No sentence about a plan, a rule or a danger may need reading twice. Few numbers: the gauge, and the ones the book is about.
- Section 10 of the bible stands. Neither woman is softened.

Then make the one change the design asks for after chapter 10, in the chapter it names, and nothing else there.

Load the `stop-slop` skill and audit each chapter before the next. Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git. Reply with one line a chapter: its word count and its last line.
