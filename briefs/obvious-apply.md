# Brief: apply the read for obvious sentences

JC stopped at "She tried her right hand first, because she was right-handed" and called it stupid. A reader then went through the whole book for sentences of that kind. The list is `audit/obvious.md`: 60 sentences, 3 joins, and a table of words. Apply it to `manuscript/`.

A chapter may have changed since the list was made. Find each sentence by its words, not by its line number. If a sentence is no longer there, skip it.

## Sentences

- Apply every fix that is not marked *judgment call*, as written.
- Apply a *judgment call* too, unless the cut would remove a line that a later chapter calls back to. "She was scared." stays in 00 and in the flood night, where it is an echo, and goes everywhere else.
- Apply the three joins.

Do the four first-use fixes under "No plainer word": landing, gutter, sash, and leave the others.

## Rules

- Change nothing else. `briefs/line.md` is the standard for any sentence you have to rejoin.
- Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.

Reply in three lines: how many sentences you changed, how many you skipped and why, and the word count before and after.
