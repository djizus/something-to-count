# Review of the outline

Accepted in substance. Three faults to fix in `outline.md`, and in `bible.md` where it says the same thing.

1. **Chapter 2 sees a day that has not happened.** Both women wake at 02:51 on Tuesday 24 November. In chapter 1 Maylis signs for the letter at about half past ten that morning and Jeannot laughs at ten past five that evening. Lesca cannot have watched either by 02:51. What reaches her on the first night is what Maylis carried to bed on Monday: the winch, the rib, or anything earlier that has Jeannot alive in it. Choose it, keep "her old waterproof" as the detail that pays in chapter 8, and check every later glimpse the same way: nothing arrives before the other one has lived it. Read `manuscript/01.md` first; it is canon now.

2. **Chapter 14 comes before the thing it depends on.** Lesca starts rehearsing "Go to the tonne first" in 14, but she learns that no flood ever came to Maylis's village in 16, and until then she believes she is watching a world where she knocked. Move the discovery into 14 or earlier, so that the sentence starts because she knows the flood there is still to come. Leave 16 the laugh and her decision to stay. Then state, where the drafting writers will see it, why she keeps rehearsing a sentence while meaning to stay in the chair.

3. **Chapter 20 is thin**, as you said. Give it a second thing that happens in the village, or shorten it and move the words to a chapter that is carrying too much. Keep the rule that each chapter from 17 on is shorter than the one before.

One thing to confirm in the bible's ending, not to change: in chapter 23 the cellar stays dry because she never opens the gate, so the walk saves a man who was in no danger and costs forty houses. If that is meant, say so in one line in section 6, so no drafting writer softens it.
