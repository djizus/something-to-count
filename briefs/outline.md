# Brief: the outline

You wrote `bible.md`. Now write `outline.md`, the chapter plan the drafting writers will work from. Read `PLAN.md` again first, since it has changed, and read `trial/deadpan.md` and `trial/dark.md` to see how chapter 1 came out. Chapter 1 is being merged from those two and is not yours to change.

## Size

- About 24 chapters, for a book of 50,000 to 55,000 words. Give each chapter a word target.
- Chapters alternate between Maylis and Lesca unless you can say why one does not.
- The book runs from Monday 23 November to Sunday 13 December 2026. Date every chapter.

## For each chapter

- Whose it is, the date, and the word target.
- What happens, in two or three lines. Events, not themes.
- What is different by the end of it.
- What it plants and what it pays off: glimpses, leaks, rungs of the craft, callbacks between the two villages, the public choices, the envelope.
- Who appears.
- The last thing that happens in it.

## Tracks

After the chapters, one table per track, each row giving the chapter where the thing is planted and the chapter where it pays:

- the six glimpses Maylis gets, and the ones Lesca gets;
- the five leaks;
- the five rungs of the craft and what each costs by day;
- the three public choices and Maylis's three moments, as each comes through to the other;
- one callback pair for each of the eight villagers;
- who learns what about the van, and when.

## Tests the outline must pass

- Every chapter has something that happens in the waking village. No chapter is only a dream.
- Every chapter has at least one thing the narrator can report with a straight face that is funny, and at least one thing that costs somebody.
- Chapters 1 and 2 together make a stranger want chapter 3.
- By the midpoint the reader knows there are two of her and has misread the reflector at least once.
- From the chapter where the Adour starts rising in Maylis's world, each chapter is shorter than the one before.
- Nothing is explained by a character who exists to explain it.

## Rules

- A working document, plain and concrete, under 3,500 words.
- Load the `stop-slop` skill and audit against it before you finish.
- If the outline forces a change to the bible, make the change in `bible.md` and list it in your reply.
- Nothing you write into this directory mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.
- Reply in five lines at most: the chapter count and total target, any change you made to the bible, and the two chapters you are least sure of.
