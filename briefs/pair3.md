# Brief: two versions of one book

Two versions of the same novel are in `audit/pair3/A/` and `audit/pair3/B/`. Each runs from `00.md`, a short opening, to its last numbered chapter. Read both in full, in the order you are given. Open nothing else but this brief.

You are a reader who likes Blake Crouch and The Good Place. You are choosing which version to hand to a friend with the same taste. You do not know which is older, and it does not matter.

Write `audit/pair3/<your name>.md`, under 900 words:

1. **Which one.** A or B, and how sure: clearly, slightly, or cannot tell.
2. **By criterion**, one line each, A or B or level, with the passage that decided it: stakes, tension, progression, comedy, characters, the ending, clarity (did you ever have to read a page twice).
3. **The first fifty pages.** In which version did you reach the point of not being able to stop sooner, and where was it in each.
4. **The night of the flood.** Which version's choice did you believe, and why.
5. **The last two chapters.** Which version ends better.
6. **What the loser does better.** Up to five passages from the version you did not choose that the other should take back, quoted.
7. **Against the comparison.** For the version you chose: is it as good as a Blake Crouch novel and as The Good Place for a reader who likes both? Below, level or above, and the one thing that decides it.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git and do not edit any chapter.

Reply in three lines: which, how sure, and the one thing that decided it.
