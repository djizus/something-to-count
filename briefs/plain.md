# Brief: the plain pass

You are a first reader and a line editor, and you have never seen this book. Do not open anything in this directory but this brief and the chapters in `manuscript/`, from `00.md` to the last. Read them in order.

Ten rounds of readers have said the same thing about this novel: the people, the comedy and the ending are very good, and too many sentences have to be read twice. The writers know the plot too well to see which. You do not know it, so you can.

## What to do

Read as a stranger reads, at speed. Each time you have to stop, go back, or work something out, that sentence is at fault. Rewrite it in place so that it reads once.

Stop for these above all:

- **Who is acting.** Two women share a name, and at times a body. Every sentence with an act in it names who does it. "She" is used only when it can mean one person.
- **When it happened.** A step back in time is marked by its tense or by a plain word of time.
- **What it means.** A sentence that says a thing by leaving it out, by a private word, by a figure of bookkeeping, or by two negatives, is said straight.
- **How the crossing works.** There are three rules. Wherever the text relies on one, a reader must be able to follow without having memorised it: say what happens in plain words at that spot.
- **What each woman is trying to do**, wherever a plan is stated, and above all in the chapters of the last night and at the choice at the gate.
- **The premise**, in the opening chapters: that there are two of her, in two versions of one village, and that the flood which came to one in 2014 is coming to the other now.

## Limits

- Change the sentence, not the scene. Facts, events, dialogue that already reads cleanly, jokes, and anything that is clear stay exactly as they are.
- Keep the narrator's voice: third person, past tense, dry, exact, British spelling. Plain does not mean flat or long. Prefer two short sentences to one that folds.
- Do not explain a joke, and do not add comment. If a dark or comic effect depends on something being left unsaid and a stranger still gets it, leave it.
- Do not change the length of any chapter by more than three per cent.

## Report

Write `audit/plain-pass.md`: for each chapter, how many sentences you changed, and the five worst as before and after. Then reply in three lines: the total, and the three kinds of sentence you had to mend most.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.
