## 1. Five rules, and no others

After this pass the book has these and a reader can say them:

1. If one of them stays in the chair when the other wakes, she wakes in the other's body and drives it. The owner keeps her right hand.
2. Inguma takes the visitor home when it has finished counting. Anybody who knows the words can give it something to count.
3. If the visitor will not be taken, she dies in her own bed, and plays bingo with the dead.
4. Lesca brings her river: every night she crosses, one more day of her flood. Friday the 11th is the last page of her file.
5. Standing up in the steel room, a woman can only say what is true.

Each is said once, by a person, where it is first needed. **Cut the rest:**
- "Only the last night counts", as a separate rule. Rule 4 covers it: if she does not cross on Friday, Friday's page never comes.
- "Sit on it and it cannot take you home." Gone from chapters 10 and 12. See section 2.
- "Lie still and you are in." Maylis pushes or she does not; nobody states it.
- Kévin counts his fingers because Kévin does. It is his habit, not a rule of the chair.
- "What one of them knows, the other gets" is shown by the card and the rib. It is not a numbered point.
- Wherever a rule is said a second time, by anybody, cut the second.
