# Brief: the repair

Four rounds of critics, three rebuilds. The people, the comedy and the ending are at the bar. Clarity is not, the middle stalls, and the two women never finish with each other. `revision4.md` is a repair, not a rebuild: the order of chapters stands and the text is edited in place. Read it in full, with the sections of `bible.md` it changed, and the reports in `audit/round4-*.md` for what each change answers.

`manuscript-v4/` is a copy of the book as the critics read it. You edit `manuscript/`.

## If you are writing new scenes

Write each new scene the plan gives you into `drafts/v5/`, named by its number. Keep to its length. Read the text either side of where it goes. Then reply with one line a scene.

## If you are editing chapters

For each of your chapters, in order:

1. Make the changes the plan lists for that chapter, and set any new scene from `drafts/v5/` in its place.
2. Apply the rules card. The crossing has the rules on the card and no others. Where the text relies on a rule that is gone, change the text.
3. Say who is driving, in one plain sentence, at the head of any chapter where a woman is in the wrong body or carries a passenger. The two are Maylis and Lesca, by name.
4. Apply the line-pass list, item by item. Any sentence about the mechanics or a plan that has to be read twice is rewritten until it can be read once. If you had to know the plan to understand a sentence, a reader cannot.
5. Touch nothing the plan protects, and nothing in a chapter marked "nothing but the line pass" beyond that pass.
6. Keep each chapter within five per cent of its present length unless the plan says otherwise.

Reply when all your chapters are done: one line a chapter with its word count and what you changed.
