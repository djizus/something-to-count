# Brief: the last contradictions

`state.md` was rebuilt from the final text, and its part 4 lists what still contradicts itself. Mend each one with the smallest change, as decided below. Everything follows `briefs/line.md`. Change nothing else.

## Facts

1. **The care home's doors.** Chapter 13 is right: the evacuation runs from the front yard and the glass doors, and the bank end is behind a fire door and down a ramp. Make chapter 11 agree: the minibus is in the front yard and Kévin is sent there. The back door, at the bank end, with the light over it, is only where Maylis and Coralie talk.
2. **Twenty-five minutes.** It is twenty-five minutes because of the mud and the water at the dip, in both villages and both years. In chapter 7 Lesca times it "on foot, in this mud", not "with this rib". In chapter 9 it is twenty-five minutes "on foot, in the dark, with the dip under water".
3. **The letter about the handle.** Jeannot put the handle in "number thirty", not number six.
4. **The town hall opens at 2.** In chapter 6, at 9 on the Tuesday morning, Larrère is in his car in front of the town hall, where he spends his mornings, and shows her the folder through the gap in the window.
5. **The huts.** There are several huts on the fields and Jeannot is the one man who still uses his. Say "still" in chapter 11.
6. **Where the river is read.** In Maylis's village it is always "the marker post". "The pillar of the old gate" belongs to Lesca's village only.
7. **Bayonne's letter.** In chapter 16 Coralie takes it from behind the fruit bowl, where it has stood since March. It was never in a wet pocket.

## Timeline

8. **The lights.** Chapter 5, Monday 30 November: the lights have been up for five days. Larrère's question through the gap is still an either-or about them that makes sense that day: whether they are switched on at 5 or at 6.
9. **Jeannot's water.** In chapter 1 it is "a hand of water" standing on the fields since the last high tide, not rainwater. The rain began on the night of the 23rd.

## Knowledge

10. **Chapter 6**, "All she knew about that night was a date": she has been told the date, the place and the hour. What she does not have is the night itself, minute by minute. Say that.
11. **Chapter 15.** Lesca left at 02:53 with the bank standing, so she cannot tell Madame Etcheto that the river came into that room. She says what she has: she took her river over there, and in the hall they called twelve and somebody sat down. "It was you" stays.

## Threads

12. **The rule about the truth**, chapter 4: "Standing up in this room, a woman can't say a thing she knows isn't true." And her ready answer stops at its first false word, not after a whole false sentence.
13. **The call on the screen in 2014.** That night the call came up on Lesca's phone as a landline number with no name. So neither she nor Coralie, reading the list of calls, knew whose kitchen it was. One line in chapter 9, and one in chapter 10 if the list is read there.

15. **"I want you at my table at 7."** In chapter 14, on the way home from Bayonne, Coralie tells Lesca her mother has had the table laid since 7.
