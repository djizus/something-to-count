# Brief: the cut

A fifth round of fresh critics read the book: `audit/round5-thriller.md`, `round5-comedy.md`, `round5-characters.md`, `round5-reader.md`, `round5-prose.md`. Read all five, and above all where each says they skimmed, doubted or put the book down.

**Where it stands after five rounds.** The scores have stopped moving. People, comedy, the switch between dark and comic and the ending sit at 7 to 9 from every reader. Tension sits at 6 and progression at 5 to 6, round after round, and every verdict is the same sentence: "I would have to tell them to hold on until chapter 15." Chapters 15 to 19 are called level with Crouch or above him. Chapters 1 to 14 are what a reader has to get through to reach them.

Four plans have tried to fix the first fourteen chapters by adding to them: scenes, rules, swaps, arguments. Each addition was good and the sentence did not change. This plan does the one thing not yet tried. **It cuts.**

The prize needs 40,000 words. The book is 57,500. A reader who is pulled through a book does not count its pages.

## What the plan must achieve

1. **The book comes down to between 44,000 and 48,000 words**, and every word of the difference comes out of chapters 1 to 14. Chapters 15 to 22 lose nothing but what the critics name.
2. **Chapters 1 to 14 become eight or nine chapters.** Keep, in each, only what a reader would miss: what the critics list as untouchable, what plants something the last third pays, and what makes a reader afraid or makes them laugh aloud. A scene that is good and does neither goes. Chapters the critics skimmed (they name 10, 12 and 14, and doubt 2 and 4) are merged into their neighbours or cut to their one necessary scene.
3. **The night at the gate begins by three fifths of the way through the book**, not four fifths.
4. **The first swap is by the fourth chapter**, and the reader knows a flood is coming to the other village by the end of the second.
5. **Chapter 1 is half its length**: the thing on the wardrobe, the postman, the eggs. A reader must laugh and be afraid inside the first thousand words.
6. **Every chapter that remains ends on a danger that has just got nearer**, and none runs past 2,300 words before chapter 15.
7. **Nothing the last third pays is left unplanted.** For each plant you cut, say where its pay-off now gets what it needs, or cut the pay-off's reliance on it.
8. **No new scenes.** Joins only, a few lines each, where two kept scenes now meet.

## Form

`revision5.md`, under 3,000 words:

- The new chapter list: for each, the scenes kept, named by present chapter and letter, in order, each with the length it is cut to, and the line it ends on.
- The cut list: by present chapter, what goes, and for anything that was a plant, where its pay-off stands now.
- The joins.
- A check: total words, the percentage at which the first swap, the knowledge of the flood, and the night at the gate now fall.

Update `bible.md` only where a fact changes.

## Rules

- What must not be touched: section 4 of each round-five report.
- Load the `stop-slop` skill. Do not run git and do not edit `manuscript/`. Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session.
- Reply in five lines at most: the new chapter count and length, the three percentages, the largest things cut, and anything you could not settle.
