# Brief: the seventh plan, clarity and the first three chapters

A seventh round read the book: `audit/round7-*.md`. Read all five. Scores are flat. Two things hold it under the bar, and every critic names them.

**1. Nobody can say how the crossing works.** Clarity is 4 to 5. The rules arrive as riddles, or in the sentence that needs them ("Maylis had been awake when Inguma pinned her, so the body was still hers. Lesca, coming in from the chair, got one arm"; "Then three's by the hour"; "Have Friday. I'll have yours"). Earlier plans added a rules card and the text still relies on more rules than the card holds.

**2. A Crouch reader is gone by chapter 3**, at the winch meeting. Chapters 11 to 15 are at the bar; a reader has to be carried to them.

Write `revision7.md`, under 2,500 words, for editors to apply in place.

## The mechanism

- **Three rules, each a sentence a child could repeat.** Write them.
- **Then go through `manuscript/` and list every sentence that states a mechanic, relies on one, or has a woman plan around one**: chapter, the sentence as it stands, and the plain sentence that replaces it. Where the plot needs a fourth rule, change the plot beat so that it does not, and say how. Counting tasks, lamps, who was awake when pinned, hours and arms: each is either one of the three or it goes.
- **The rules are said aloud once, early, by someone who understands them, to someone who does not**, and are said again in a line just before each time they matter: before the first ride, before the marriage scene, before the last night.
- **On the eve of the night the reader can state each woman's plan.** One plain sentence each, in her own chapter, and the two plans visibly collide.
- **The river crossing the red line happens on the page**, not in the pluperfect.

## The first three chapters

- A reader must be unable to stop at the end of each of the first three chapters. Say what each ends on now and what it ends on after.
- Chapter 3 loses the winch meeting as a scene, or keeps the forty seconds of it that are funny. The first ride moves into chapter 3 if that is what it takes.
- By the end of chapter 3 the reader has: the thing on the wardrobe, two of her, a flood coming on a date, and one woman inside the other.

## Also

- Lesca's reckoning is on the page: the consequences now postponed to "a Monday and a Tuesday" are shown, at least one of them.
- Cut each place the narrator names what a scene has just shown. The critics list them.

## Rules

- What must not be touched: section 4 of each round-seven report.
- Length 44,000 to 47,000 words. Load the `stop-slop` skill. Do not run git or edit `manuscript/`. Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session.
- Reply in five lines: the three rules, what chapters 1 to 3 now end on, and anything you could not settle.
