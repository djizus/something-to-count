# Brief: two rules fewer, and the first one closed by who she is

Every reader now names one thing: "the rulebook, about a dozen by chapter 9". Two of those rules were added in the last passes and can go without losing what they bought. You are the only writer. Everything follows `briefs/line.md` and JC's notes on reading.

## 1. Chapter 9: the words fail because she says "Either"

At present Maylis says Madame Etcheto's words in full, gives Inguma a long count, and it sits on her anyway. Lesca then explains a rule: the count only decides when the visitor is taken home, it does not keep the thing off, and 2:51 is her minute. That is a new rule two chapters from the flood, and it also spends the best moment in chapter 13, where Maylis says the words for the first time in her life with no "Either" after them.

The book already knows why the words do not work for her. In chapter 2 she says them, has to choose what it should count, and says "Either". So:

- **She tries the words again, and the same thing happens.** She says "Inguma, enauk hire beldur." Then she has to give it something to count, and there are two things she could give it, and what comes out of her is "Either". It sits on her. Play it as the scene it is: short, and funny until it lands.
- In the steel room she tells Lesca she said the words. Lesca asks what she gave it to count. That is the whole exchange. Lesca does not explain a rule.
- Cut "The count tells it when to take the visitor home… on your kitchen floor", "the words don't keep it off", and anything else in the book that says the words cannot keep it off. When Maylis lists why she will sit up in the van, it is because she cannot push and she cannot give it its work.
- Chapter 13 is unchanged, and now it is the first time.
