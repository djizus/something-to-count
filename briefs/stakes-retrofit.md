# Stakes retrofit for merged chapters

Two changes from JC, set out in sections 4 and 6 of the bible. Coralie was on night duty at the home on the flood night of 2014, and Lesca knew it. On the last night in Maylis's village, Madame Etcheto drowns in her bed. This file lists what changes in chapters already merged. Nothing before chapter 19 changes except three plants. Suggested wording is a guide and gets rewritten in the book's voice.

## Plants

### Chapter 7

- Where the second duck goes "to the table by the radiators, where Coralie held the card up for the winner", name the winner. She is Madame Etcheto, who is ninety-four, and she has the duck put on her knees.
- In Coralie's line at the bar, "The one who won the duck asked me if there'd be a loto next year" becomes "Madame Etcheto asked me if there'd be a loto next year". The answer stays.

### Chapter 15

- At the tractor, where "Faces came to the windows of the home, in a row, and stayed there", one of them is hers. Madame Etcheto is at the end window on the ground floor, and her lips are moving. Maylis does not hear what she is saying. Nothing else is added.

## Chapter 19

One change, which is where Coralie was that night, and three sentences that follow from it.

- "It began with a telephone, in a bed, in the dark. Coralie was asleep against the wall" becomes a bed with its wall side empty. Coralie was on nights at the home that week.
- "The home had a light on the stairs" gains her car in the yard. She was somewhere under that light, with forty-two people in their beds.
- At the gauge, "the beds of the home" gains what was in them, people who could not get out of them.
- In Maylis's reckoning afterwards, after "had chosen", one sentence. She had the forty houses and she had the car in the yard of the home, and nothing in what came told her which of them had turned the handle.

Everything else in the chapter stands. "Not a house in the Port had water in it, and nobody behind any shutter had woken" is true and stays. Maylis's own Coralie already says she is on nights on Friday.

## Chapter 20

The council scene stands, with "forty houses" as Lesca's phrase. The scene on the landing is reworked from Coralie's answer to "Why not?".

- **Cut.** "I worked nights at the home then. I'd put thirty of them to bed that evening, behind that bank. I knew by breakfast what you'd saved. I didn't want to know what you'd known." It puts her at home in bed by the time of the call, and that is no longer so.
- **In its place.** She was in there. She was on nights that week, and the rota was on the fridge, where Lesca had read it. There were two of them on, forty-two beds, and nine residents who could have walked out. At half past two there was water under the back door and she was laying towels. By four it had stopped coming. At breakfast she was told what had been done, and at nine she picked up the telephone.
- **Why she never asked.** She has had two answers for twelve years. Either it was opened for forty houses, and she lives with the mayor. Or it was opened because she was in that building, and then the man is hers as well, and she has stood beside his widow for eleven Decembers. She has never wanted to hear which, and she tells Lesca not to say it now. Keep "I wanted you in the house" and the landing between them.
- **Lesca.** She says "It rang once", as written. She does not say which. The narration does not say it for her.
- **Small fit.** "You came in at nine" needs Coralie home before her. She came off at seven.
- **Stands.** The call log read off like a chart, the minute said aloud, Lesca looking at the top of the wardrobe, "Then there's two", the sentence said at the sink, the speech left unaltered, and the bull.

## Checked and left alone

- Chapter 10, the citation, already says "forty dwellings, a care home and the lives of those within them".
- Chapter 10, "She had made a habit of not asking about that winter, and Lesca had always put it down to tact", is the plant for chapter 20 and stays.
- Chapter 16, "forty houses and a care home" and "She had put forty houses against one man, once, and had been right", are the sum she repeats. They stay as hers.
- Chapters 4 and 17 already put forty-two beds behind the bank.
- No merged chapter says that the home was empty or that nobody was in danger on either night.
