## Times, spelling, thoughts

| Old | New |
|---|---|
| any time in narration | words: "nine minutes to three in the morning" |
| a clock or screen being read | digits: 02:51 |
| dates | "Tuesday the twenty-fourth of November 2026" |
| flashback in past perfect | one marker ("Twelve years earlier, in June 2014"), then simple past; return with "Now" |
| spelling | British: grey, centimetres, towards, windscreen. Single quotation marks. The words above stand even where American |
| thoughts | plain third person, past tense: "She was scared." No italics; italics are for written words only (the pad, letters). Contractions in dialogue and in her thoughts |
