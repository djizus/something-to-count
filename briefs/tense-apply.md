# Brief: apply the tense read

JC stopped at "Lesca lay on her back in a bed that wasn't hers and listened…" and asked whether the tenses were wrong. They were right, and they read as wrong. Three proofreaders then read every verb in the book for the same thing: `audit/tense-one.md` (chapters 00 to 05), `audit/tense-two.md` (06 to 11), `audit/tense-three.md` (12 to 17). Each entry quotes the sentence and gives a corrected one. Apply them.

Writer one has chapters 00 to 08. Writer two has chapters 09 to 17.

- **Apply every correction for your chapters.** Find each sentence by its words. If it is no longer there, skip it.
- **"lay" and "lain" as the past of "lie" leave the book.** After you have applied the reports, search your chapters for both words and mend any the reports missed. "lay" as the present or base form of "to lay" stays ("They lay a place for her", "will lay the flowers"), and so do "laid", "lie", "lying" and Kévin's "Lie still under it".
- **One phrase for the book's central action.** Where a woman does not move under Inguma, the narration says "kept still" every time ("She kept still under it", "Maylis had kept still for the village's sake"). Not "stayed still" in one place and "didn't move" in another.
- **Use the proofreader's sentence** unless it changes what happens, changes a fact, breaks a joke, or reads worse than the original. In that case write your own sentence that answers the same complaint. A contraction in speech is expanded only where the report shows it can be read two ways.
- Three decisions that differ from the reports:
  - Chapter 1, "So had the stone." → "The stone had come up to the same place."
  - Chapter 1, "The L cut in the stone wouldn't run." → "The L in the stone wouldn't run."
  - Chapter 12, Larrère: "'I'd be up that lane every night of the year if she let me,' Larrère said."
- Change nothing else. Keep the plain style of `briefs/line.md`: short sentences, common words, and no two sentences joined by ", and" unless they are parts of one action. Inguma is "it", always.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.

Reply in three lines: how many corrections you applied, which you changed or skipped and why, and the word count before and after.
