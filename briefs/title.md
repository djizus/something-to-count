# Brief: the title

You have just read the novel. Its working title is *Either*. The author is not satisfied with it. Answer from your read alone, in under 300 words, in the file you are given.

1. **Is *Either* the right title?** Yes or no, and one sentence. Say what a person who has not read the book would expect from that word on a cover, in a list of forty other entries for a prize.
2. **Rank these nine**, best first, with a few words on the top three and on the bottom one:
   - *Either*
   - *The Keyholder*
   - *The Gate Stays Shut*
   - *Something to Count*
   - *Forty-One*
   - *The Other One*
   - *Somebody Has to Stack the Chairs*
   - *Both*
   - *The Second Shovel*
3. **Two titles of your own**, taken from words or things that are in the book, each with one sentence on why. A title must not give away the ending, must be easy to say and to remember, and must suit a book that is funny and then is not.
4. **Your one choice** from everything above.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git and do not edit anything else.

Reply in one line: your one choice.
