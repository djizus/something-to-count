# Brief: check the lines added in the prose pass

`audit/voice-changes.txt` lists every sentence that changed in the last pass, by chapter: a line starting "-" is the old text and the line under it starting "+" is the new. About forty-five short lines were added and about twenty-five cut. Read `state.md` parts 1 to 3. Then find each added or changed line in its chapter in `manuscript/` and read the paragraph around it.

For each added line, check:

1. **Fact.** Does it bring in a number, an object, a person or an event that disagrees with `state.md` or with another chapter? (For example a count of seats, bags, kilos, metres, years or trips; who has what; what happened before.) Check every figure by doing the sum.
2. **Language.** A grammar slip, a pronoun whose owner is not clear at once, "lay" or "lain" as the past of "lie", a time or date not in figures.
3. **Reads twice.** Would a fluent reader whose first language is not English have to read it twice to know what it means or why it is there?
4. **Whose line.** Maylis is an outdoor worker who thinks in jobs, tools, loads and time a job takes. Lesca is a mayor who thinks in items, votes and minutes. Is the line in the head of the right woman for that scene?

For each cut, check that nothing after it now refers to something the reader was not told.

Write `audit/voice-check.md`: only the lines that fail, each quoted with its chapter, which check it fails, and the smallest mend (or "cut it"). If a line is fine, do not list it. Give the count at the top.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git and do not edit the manuscript.

Reply in two lines: how many lines fail, and the worst.
