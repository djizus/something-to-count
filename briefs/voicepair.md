# Brief: two tellings of the same two chapters

You are given a folder with two sub-folders, X and Y. Each holds chapter 1 and chapter 3 of the same novel. The story, the speech and the facts are the same in both. Only the telling differs, in some sentences. Chapter 1 is in the head of Maylis, a village's outdoor worker. Chapter 3 is in the head of another woman, Lesca, a mayor.

Open nothing but this brief and your folder. Read X's two chapters, then Y's two chapters, in full. You do not know which was written first, and you are not to guess.

You will be told which reader you are.

- **The fluent reader.** English is your second language. You read Harlan Coben and Blake Crouch in English without going back up the page. You want a book that reads as easily as theirs.
- **The juror.** You are widely read in literary fiction and you sit on a prize jury. You are listening for a voice: whether somebody is telling this, and whether the telling of chapter 1 and the telling of chapter 3 belong to two different heads.

Write your report to the file you are given, under 700 words:

1. **Ease.** In each version, every sentence you had to read twice, quoted. Which version reads more easily, X, Y or the same?
2. **Voice.** In which version can you tell from the telling alone whose head you are in? Quote two sentences that do it.
3. **Sentences that read as wrong.** In either version: a sentence that sounds forced, decorated, turned around for no reason, joined to another it has nothing to do with, or that explains what was plain. Quote each and say which version it is in.
4. **The differences.** Of the sentences that differ, the three you would most want kept and the three you would most want changed back, quoted, with the version.
5. **Your choice.** X or Y for the whole book, and how sure you are from 1 to 5. One sentence on why.

Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git and do not edit anything.

Reply in two lines: your choice with how sure you are, and the one sentence you would most want changed back.
