# Brief: twelve people, twelve ways of being funny

For four rounds the comedy reader has said one thing, and the characters and prose readers now say it too: "Everybody is witty in the same shape." "Seven mouths, one joke-form: the short, literal comeback that ends the exchange." "The women of the village speak in one voice." The Good Place has people who are wrong in different voices; no line of theirs could be given to another.

Read `audit/round25-comedy.md` section 2 and faults 2, 4, 8 and 9; `audit/round25-characters.md` faults 2 and 5; `audit/round24-comedy.md` faults 1, 2 and 7; `audit/round25-prose.md` fault 2. Then read the whole book, `manuscript/00.md` to `16.md`. You are the only writer on this pass, and you touch speech, and the line or two of narration around a speech, and nothing else.

## What to do

1. **Ration the dry retort.** Go through every scene. Where anybody but Coralie or Madame Etcheto ends an exchange on the short literal comeback, cut it, or let the person say it their own way. This is mostly deletion.
2. **Ration the diagnosis.** Maylis's flaw is named by Coralie in chapter 1, by Sarthou's joke, on the platform in 11 and in "Either, with the river in it." Cut it from Arlette, Lesca, Jeannot and the narrator. Lesca's counting is named once, by Darrigrand on the bank; cut the other three.
3. **Two-in-one-body gags are readable at once.** Before each one, the reader knows who wants what. No sentence where the reader must work out whose intention failed.
4. **Give Lesca's village something to laugh at after chapter 6.** The rehearsal in chapter 10 is twelve lines; it can carry more.
5. **Where the narrator says a joke before or after the person does, cut the narrator's.** Where people on the page laugh harder than the line earns, cut the laughing.
6. You may add up to 600 words in the whole book, in speech. Every added line belongs to one person in the table and could not be given to another.

## What you do not touch

Section 4 of every report in `audit/round23-*.md`, `round24-*.md` and `round25-*.md`. The rules of the chair and the turn in chapters 7 and 8, which another pass has just set. The night of the flood from "02:53" to "Forty-one".

## Rules

- The voice is `briefs/style.md`, `briefs/style-words.md` and `briefs/rhythm.md`.
- A joke is never explained, by the narrator or by the next speaker.
- The dark scenes stay dark.
- Nothing you write mentions any writing tool, model, vendor or session. Do not run git.

Reply in eight lines at most: for each person in the table, how many lines you cut and how many you added, and the three additions you are surest of, quoted.
