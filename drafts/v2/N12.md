# N12, chapter 11, comic. Joins: after 13b, before 13c, which opens with her at the counter.

* * *

At two on Friday Maylis followed Madame Ducasse, the secretary, into the mairie, went round the end of the counter and through the door behind it, and sat down at the mayor's desk.

It was the chair with arms. She moved his tray a hand to the left and took his pen from its stand. Her right hand was in its bandage. She uncapped the pen with her teeth, held it in the left over the first letter in the tray, and read the heading.

Madame Ducasse stood in the doorway with her coat on. She had kept that counter since 2003, and in that time nobody had come round it.

Guy Larrère came in at five past with his folder and stopped beside her.

'You have three minutes,' Maylis said.

She heard it as it left her. She put the cap back on with her teeth, stood the pen in its stand and got up out of the chair by stages, with her forearm against her side.

Larrère looked at the tray.

'Madame Ducasse. Is this lady in the day book?'

'She's the commune's employee, Monsieur le maire. She has a key.'

'She's on my side of the counter, at my desk. That's a caller. Enter her. The time, the name, and what she came about.'

'What did she come about?'

They both waited for her.

'The school roof,' Maylis said.

'Then she can ask for it at the counter,' Larrère said, 'in opening hours.'

Madame Ducasse wrote it. Maylis went back round the end of the counter, to the public side, and stood at it.
