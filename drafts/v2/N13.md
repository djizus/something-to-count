Lesca wrote before she moved. She set it down as findings, and numbered them.

*1. When the other one gets up and I am still sitting, I go with her.*

*2. I have her bed and her eyes, and she is still in them. I told the right hand to open. It opened, and she was trying to shut it.*

*3. While I am there, my own bed holds a woman with her eyes open who cannot be woken. Coralie timed twenty-two minutes.*

*4. Inguma fetched me back. If it had not, there would be one of us, with both in her. The hand would go to whichever held on longer.*

She read the four down. The second was the one she had not known she would have to write.
