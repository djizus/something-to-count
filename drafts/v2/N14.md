Then she put down what she would take, since a motion that listed only what it left was half a motion.

She would take a rib, the seventh on the right. She would take a village with Jeannot Castagnet in it, owing her for diesel. She would take half a hand, since the other one would be in the fingers as well, and on the night of the gate there would be two of them on one handle.

She had a column for each of those.

For one item she could find no column. It was the woman whose bed it was. Lesca had put a man in a scale before now. This one had her own hands, and would be awake in them. She left the line open and went on.
