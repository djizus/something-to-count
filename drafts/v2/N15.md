The pen went on across the page. Maylis told the hand to stop, as a person does who finds her own fingers writing in a script that leans. The pen dragged. The line went crooked for the width of three squares, down and to the left, and then the other one bore down, brought it straight and finished the date.

* * *

Against the sash and that pen she set one thing, on the last line of the form. If she stayed gone, there would be a woman in this bed with her eyes open, and it would be Coralie who came in at six and found her.
