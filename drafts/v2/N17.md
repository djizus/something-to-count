'Then I'll give you one item of it now,' she said. 'I've not got it straight, so you'll have it crooked. Over there it began to rain on the twenty-fourth of November. That was the first night I woke with something that wasn't mine. It was the first night Inguma sat on the two of us at one minute. I've had one night in my head for twelve years, Father, with nineteen days of rain in front of it. I thought it was only me that had it. I think it was being kept on my side, and I think I've let it across. The river there is doing what mine did, to the day.'

She waited for him to tell her that weather is not carried from one bed to another.

'I baptise with water,' Père Honoré said. 'I do not know where it has been.'

He did not say that she was wrong.
