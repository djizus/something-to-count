# N18, chapter 17, comic. Joins: in 19d, after "He was out of doors. She could hear rain on a hood, and water." It replaces the exchange and the reported swearing, and "She listened to all of it." follows.

* * *

'Nobody. It's Maylis Lesca.'

'Then I'll number it for you, since you'll want it in a letter. One. It is five in the morning. Two. I'd three hundred teal sat on a hand of water that I waited a fortnight for, and your telephone has put up every bird of them, and they'll not come down again before Sunday. Three is the commune. It drains me on a Monday, it sends a girl to my kitchen with a pot on a Sunday, and at five on a Friday it rings a man in a hole in a meadow to ask is he in. Four is your father. He stopped four turns short for thirty years and never rang me once, he'd have cut the hand off first, and you've his key and none of his sense. Five is the association, and for that I go back to 1962, when they dug their gate where my grandfather had his hide and gave the handle to a Larrère.' He drew breath. 'And six.'

Six was two words. She had last heard them from him across a grave.
