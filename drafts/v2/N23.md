At twenty metres Maylis turned, unhooked the key from her belt and threw it back along the crown.

At two hundred metres the crown ended. It had been cut through. Where the road came up from the Port there was a gap the width of a house, with the river going through it in a slope, brown and smooth, and breaking at the bottom on what was left of Sarthou's hedge.
