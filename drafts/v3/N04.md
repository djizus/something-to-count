# N4, chapter 5, comic. Joins: in 05a, after "The association resolved, by seven votes, to seek an estimate."

* * *

'One thing, for the estimate,' Larrère said through the gap. 'The pawl by itself, or a new winch. Which does the keyholder want?'

Seven men turned to hear. It was the first question the association had ever put to Maylis.

'Either,' she said.

'The keyholder has no preference,' Larrère said, and the member who kept the book wrote that down. They asked for a price on the pawl, which was the cheaper.
