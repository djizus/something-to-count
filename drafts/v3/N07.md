# N7, chapter 7, comic then dark. Joins: after 07e, at the hall door, before the minibus is loaded. This is where the reflector arrives, awake.

* * *

When the hall was emptying Jeannot Castagnet backed his white van up to the door for the ham, and Maylis was in the doorway with a crate.

He came back slowly, in the rain, with the light of the hall on his rear doors. Low on the left-hand one was a reflector. It was round, the kind that belongs on a trailer, and it was screwed on crooked.

She saw it twice over. One reflector was in front of her, in the light of a door. The other lay across it, red, at the end of a track, in headlamps, at night, in rain. She stood with the crate against her and the two of them coming at her, one inside the other, and the van's horn sounded.

'Am I to load it through you?' Jeannot said, out of his window. 'It's a ham. It goes in the back, and you're stood in the back.'

She moved. He carried the ham past her in both arms and laid it on a blanket.

Kévin Labat was at the bar, in off the tide.

'The other one,' Maylis said, and set the crate down. 'When they gave her the medal, the square lowered its head for somebody. I've seen who. It was his van in her headlamps, at the end of a track. He's dead there. She drove into him.'

Kévin looked down the hall at Jeannot, who was shutting his doors on a ham.

'He looks all right from here.'

'Not here.'

'No.' He shrugged, and it did not come off. 'I gave you a way to tell if you're asleep, Maylis. I never said look.'
