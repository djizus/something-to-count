# N12, chapter 13, comic. Joins: in 13b, after Maïté stands the pot in the sink.

* * *

From where Maylis stood she could see the drainer. Two bowls were drying on it, and two spoons, and it was half past ten on a Sunday with the husband at the café since eight. On the dresser, beside the bread, lay a folder. It was buff, with a darker patch where rain had been at it. She had seen that folder held over a head, crossing the square, on most wet mornings.

Maïté moved the bread onto it.
