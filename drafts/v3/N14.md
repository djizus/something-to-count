# N14, chapter 14, comic. Joins: in 14b, in place of the paragraph about the pay slip and the diesel, before "She laughed."

* * *

Lesca gave the minute to the forty houses, as she did each year, and that Monday a letter came up through them. It was from over there, numbered seventeen in the corner, and she knew it by heart without having read it. *I did not ask to be pulled out. I was waiting for the ground to harden, which it would have done by March. The commune's tractor has left two ruts in my track, a forearm deep. I enclose my bill for them. I have taken nothing off for the tow, since I ordered none.*
