She went back to the head of the page and wrote the motion in.

*That the mayor remain seated.*

She read it as the prefecture would have read it. A motion states its object, and this one kept its object back. She had sent Guy Larrère's motions down the table again for less.

That morning she had turned over a hand that belonged to another woman and held Coralie's fingers in it, for the first time since a February. She had come back wanting that, and a seat was the least of it.

She made the full stop into a comma, and went on in the same ink to the end of the line.

*That the mayor remain seated, and take the other one's bed, and Coralie Mounet in it.*
