# N16, chapter 14, comic then dark. Joins: with 14d, that evening, before the motion is written in.

* * *

That evening Lesca crossed the yard to her mother's kitchen with a question, and put it before she sat down.

'The words. When your mother said them, how long did they keep Inguma off?'

Arlette was at the range. 'As long as what she gave it. The words are only a job of work. She gave it the grass on the commons, blade by blade, and it was at that till light.' She turned the potatoes. 'Madame Etcheto, down at the home, gives it the stars. Coralie tells me she rings for the night staff at three. Stars, in December, under cloud. It wants more than that, a great deal, and small. I'm not asking what you want kept off, or for how long.'

'A day. The whole of one. The gravel company sends its loads up through the square six days a week, and has since the summer of 2019. Each load goes over the weighbridge, so the loads are in a book. I rang for the figure this afternoon. I can't give it the stones. I can give it the loads and tell it to do the stones itself.'

Arlette set the pan down. It was her grazing in those lorries. She had counted her ponies up a ramp to make room for the hole.

'Say them, then,' she said. 'I'll not say them for you.'

Lesca had kept those words out of her mouth for twelve years. She said them standing, to the dark of the window.

'Inguma, enauk hire beldur. You'll count every load of gravel that has left that lake since 2019. Stone by stone, each load. You're not to come till you have.'

Arlette looked at her for some time.

'That's not for a night's sleep,' she said. 'Nobody gives it that much to sleep. I'm not asking, Maylis.'

She put a plate in front of her, and stood by the table until it was eaten.
