# N18, chapter 15, comic. Joins: after 15c, before 15f.

* * *

At noon Maylis knocked on the window of Kévin Labat's van, behind the hall, and he let her in out of the rain without sitting up.

'She was in my bed at six,' Maylis said. 'She said a thing to Coralie with my mouth. She had my hand open, and I couldn't shut it.'

He sat up.

'No,' he said. 'That can't be done. There are rules.' He found his telephone and held it up. 'There's thousands on that forum, and some of them have been at it since before I was born. Three things they all agree. Nobody stays past the alarm. When it goes you're out, there's nothing to hold on to. Nobody moves another person's hand. It's your dream and there's only you in it, so whose hand would it be? And the thing on your chest always fetches you. You couldn't stay in one if you wanted to.'

'I held on past the alarm last week. So did she.'

'That's one gone.'

'She moved my hand.'

'That's two.' He scratched his head with the telephone. 'I'll have to put it up. They'll say I made it up. There's a man in Limoges on there who says he's been to the moon, and they were kinder to him than they'll be to me.'

'There's a hall, past the room,' she said. 'The loto, with the tables going on, and everybody in it a little more than one. And a chair kept.'

He gave that longer than he gave most things. He shrugged first, to be rid of it, and it stayed.

'I'd say it's the loto before the priest has called a number. Everybody's sat down with every card they could have had. Your professor opened the door and counted one of you. That's looking. In there nobody's looked yet, so they've all still got their other one on them. Your mother. Mine. Castagnet, alive and drowned.'

'And the chair?'

'That's yours, with your card on it. Sit down in that and you're not hers and you're not yours. You're nobody's. There's nobody to fetch a person back out of there.' He lay down again. 'I wouldn't sit in it for a ham.'

'And the third rule?'

'That one holds,' Kévin said. 'It always fetches you. I'd put the van on it.'
