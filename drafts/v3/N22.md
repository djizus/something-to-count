# N22, chapter 18, comic. Joins: as the chapter's first scene.

* * *

Lesca came back to her own desk at noon on Wednesday, in the chair with arms, holding a pen she had not picked up.

Madame Ducasse stood on the far side of the desk with a folder, and laid things out of it one at a time.

'Three letters, signed this morning. I've held them.' Each had *M. Lesca* at the foot, with a tall L. 'I took it that Madame le maire would want to sign them herself.'

'I did sign them.'

'Yes, Madame le maire.' She laid down a page from a diary. 'From Monsieur Tastet.'

*Wednesday, ten past seven, Mme Lesca's kitchen. Asked whether she will speak at the stone or stand, the mayor says: Either. Taken as consent to stand. Two members present, and her mother.*

'He has sent a copy to the prefecture, so that the order of ceremony can be cut.'

Lesca read *Either* in her deputy's hand. It was a word she had never used.

'And Tuesday,' Madame Ducasse said. 'Madame Mounet telephoned at eight to say that the mayor was prevented. She used the word. So Monsieur Tastet was mayor from nine. He signed the post. He gave Madame Labat the hall keys without a form. He sat in that chair to see how it was.'

'Did he.'

'I minuted it. Monsieur Larrère asked for the whole of Tuesday to be minuted, and this morning as well, with the rake.'

'What rake?'

'You took it from Kévin Labat at nine and did the gratings. The café says you've improved.'

The safe stood open behind her. The sash lay in its box, folded in four. For eleven years she had folded it in three, along the white.

She shook it out and folded it again. Her right hand helped. It was the first thing in that office to do as she told it since Monday.
