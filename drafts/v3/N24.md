# N24, chapter 20, comic. Joins: in 19a, after "The keyholder. At Peyrebarthe." and before "Has your mayor made the request?"

'Is this a request, or a notification?' the woman said. 'I've a tray for each, and a book.'

'Either.'

'Then it's neither. A request comes from your mayor, and a notification comes from Pau, and you're the one with the key.' She waited on the line. 'I can't put Either in a tray. Shall we begin again?'
