He took a buff folder off the passenger seat and held it against the wheel, shut.

'I'll be brief. You've the look of somebody who means to use her judgement.' He tapped the tab with her name on it. 'There's a delivery slip in here, signed as a mayor signs. There's an order for a winch part on the commune's form, in that same hand, with the commune's stamp under it. You sat at my desk to do that. And I can add four winters of nets, at a platform you hold the key to. They tell me there was a net in that sluice on Wednesday night.'

'The winch needed the part.'

'I'm sure it did. It's the forging of a public document all the same, and Pau won't ask what the pawl was like.' He laid the folder back on the seat. 'If that gate moves tonight before I ring, this goes to Pau on Monday, registered. They'd suspend you inside the week. Hontaube takes on the staff of this commune as it stands on the thirty-first, and it doesn't take a file that's with the prefect. You'd have eighteen years of pension in a commune that stopped existing at midnight. You could write to it.'

She had a form for road signs in her chest pocket, with a line on the back of it about a sash.

'Is that advice?'

'No,' Guy Larrère said. 'That's the file.'
