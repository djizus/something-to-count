# N4, chapter 3, comic. Joins: after 03d, as the chapter's last scene.

* * *

At ten Maylis lay on her back and said the words to the top of the wardrobe, low, so as not to wake Coralie.

'Inguma, enauk hire beldur.'

It sounded like something read off a label. Then it needed its work. She had given it the leaves of the square in room twelve, and Madame Etcheto had said that those would do for one night. But Maylis had raked the square that afternoon, down to the gratings, and there were none left to count.

That left what the old woman gave it herself. There were stars, with the cloud down, and there was grass.

'Either,' Maylis said.

It had nothing to start on. At nine minutes to three a sack was let down off the wardrobe onto the boards.
