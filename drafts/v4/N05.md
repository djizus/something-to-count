That night she lay still under Inguma, and she was in the chair.

The panel was in front of her, and the shelf under it. A brown envelope lay on the shelf with the card drawn half out. She read the card, looked away, and read it again.

*right*

It was in her own hand. The arms of the chair were warm, and she had not sat there long enough to warm them.

Coralie's alarm rang, a long way off. It took her up out of the seat, and the seat stayed full behind her. Somebody had kept it.

Then there was the lampshade, and two fingers on the inside of her wrist. Under Coralie's fingers her right hand turned over, and she had not told it to.
