'There is more than there was, Father,' she said. 'There's another Peyrebarthe, with a river in it. Yesterday and again this morning that river stood at the reading in my file of 2014, to the centimetre. It has a fortnight to run to the night of the eleventh. I have carried that flood for twelve years and told it to you once, and I think I've let it across. It's mine.'

She waited to be told that weather is not carried from one bed to another.

'I baptise with water,' Père Honoré said. 'I do not know where it has been.'
