# N12, chapter 9, comic. Joins: after 09f, as the chapter's last scene. Its first exchange steps back into Sarthou's kitchen.

* * *

Maylis had tried Sarthou with Friday week before she signed his form.

'On the eleventh, at night, the river is ninety over the line, and that bank goes at your hedge.'

'On what information?'

She had no name to give him that was not her own.

'I record occurrences,' Sarthou had said. 'A flood on Friday week has not occurred. When it occurs it will be recorded. That is the order in which it is done.'

At home she rang the number on the prefecture's yellow sheet. A woman's voice thanked her for calling the flood watch at Pau. For a flood now in progress she was to press one. For damage from a flood that had ended she was to press two.

Maylis pressed nothing. It was neither, and it was ten days off.

'We did not recognise your choice,' the voice said, and gave her the two again.

That was everybody whose job it was. She priced what was left, and it came to one woman, over there, who signed things and was listened to.

At ten she said the words to the top of the wardrobe.

'Inguma, enauk hire beldur. You'll count the kernels on every card Marie-Jo has printed for the loto. Each card, each square. You're not to come till you've done.'
