# N16, chapter 12, comic. Joins: as the chapter's first scene.

* * *

On Friday morning Maylis turned the shopping pad over and wrote a handover on the back of it, for the relief.

*Friday 11th, night. The gate.*

*Key on the belt, left side. Handle in the box on the platform, same key.*

*It stops at nineteen. Left shoulder under it and push with the legs. Not the right hand. You know why.*

*Gauge is on the pier. The red line is the doorsteps.*

*He parks at the end of the track, a kilometre out. White Trafic. He won't have the telephone on him. Twenty-five minutes on foot.*

*Larrère rings. It needn't be answered. You call him Guy, so you'll manage.*

*Coralie is on nights at the home that week. Thirty metres from the patch.*

*If it comes through at the patch, the bank has ten minutes. You'll know that better than I do.*

She read it through. It was a night's work for one woman who had done it before, and Maylis priced it as the commune did, at forty euros for the month. She put that at the foot, and *paid*.

Then she laid the pad face up, with the boxes showing.
