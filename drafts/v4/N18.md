# N18, chapter 14, comic. Joins: as the chapter's first scene.

* * *

On Monday at six Lesca's legs got out of bed without her.

She had not sent them. She was carried across the boards to the window, where her own right hand, with the seam in it, put the curtain back to look at the frost. Maylis had come home with her, and meant to stay. Lesca got one question out between the teeth.

'How long?'

Her mouth answered her, in the higher voice.

'I gave it the grass. Every blade the commune's mower has cut since 2008.' The mouth priced it. 'The verges, the cemetery, the strip at the mairie, twice a month in the season. It'll be at that till dinner.'

Then the legs went down to the kitchen, to see what a mayor had for breakfast.
