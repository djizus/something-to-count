She tried to say it aloud, with the door across the landing open.

'I opened it for forty houses.'

It was a sentence out of a minute. She had said it too often to hear whether it was true.

'I opened it for Coralie.'

That came out no truer, and no less.

She had the sum. It was forty houses, forty-two beds, a car in the yard of the home and a van at the end of a track, with ten minutes against twenty-five. She went behind the sum for the woman who had made it, and found a handle, turning. She had the sum and nothing else.
