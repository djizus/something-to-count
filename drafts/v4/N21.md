# N21, chapter 17, comic then dark. Joins: in 20b, in place of the exchange about the signs from "The signs," to "it does it in its last month." Larrère's order and the folder follow as they stand.

* * *

'The signs,' he said. 'Pau wanted the form by today.'

Maylis took the form out of her chest pocket. The back of it had two lines on it. She held it against the roof of his car, put her pencil through the lower line, which was hers, and passed it in at the gap.

He counted the strokes on the front. Then he turned it over and read what was not struck out.

*That the mayor remain seated, and take the other one's bed, and Coralie Mounet in it.*

'The mayor is seated,' he said. 'He's in this car. And he has a bed. Whose hand is that?'

'It's on the commune's form.'

'So it is.' He opened the buff folder on the passenger seat and laid the form in it, face down, on a letter in her writing that had no date at the top. 'That makes four. If that gate moves tonight before I ring, the letter gets Monday's date.'
