# N29, chapter 22, comic then dark. Two parts. Part one joins in 25b after Sarthou lowers the telephone, in place of his charge of the kitchen and Larrère's wordless turn. Part two joins among the houses, in place of Lafitte's apology. Marie-Jo's "Nobody laughed" in the kept text will need to change.

* * *

Larrère had carried the buff folder down through the silt at the height of his shoulder, and it was dry.

He gave the gendarme no answer. He opened the folder on his forearm, took out a letter in Maylis's writing and held it up for her, as Sarthou held the telephone.

It had a date on it now. *Saturday 12 December 2026* stood at the top, in his hand, over her tall L.

'Received and accepted yesterday,' he said. 'I told you what would date it. They tell me that gate never moved. You did, which comes to the same.' He put the letter back. 'You're nobody's this morning. The key goes in my tray.'

'The file contains a second name,' Sarthou said. He said it to her, with his eyes on his notebook. 'The keyholder was under an order to remain at the gate. At 02:57 she left it. That is recorded. I do not record that I would have stayed.'

Larrère turned round in the silt, with the folder held up out of it, and went back to his car.

* * *

Madame Darrigrand stood on the pavement beside the pram, with the baby inside her coat. The pram was full of silt to the hood.

'You had the key,' she said.

'Yes.'

'I'm saying it to you. I'd sooner you had it from me than from the café.' She wiped the handle of the pram with her sleeve. 'Sarthou carried her out over his head. You were a kilometre off, fetching a man out of a dry hole.'

Maylis paced it, from the hatch to that door. It was nearer a kilometre and two hundred. She left it uncorrected.

The café door stood open as she went by. Somebody inside said that the Lesca girl had gone for a walk. Somebody laughed, once, and was told to shut up, and it was too late for that.
