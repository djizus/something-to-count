Lesca sat down at the kitchen table with the writing pad, and wrote with Maylis's left hand.

*To the Prefect, at Pau. The undersigned holds the key to the sluice gate at Peyrebarthe. The Adour has risen at that gate each day since 24 November. By its readings it will be at the line of the doorsteps on Tuesday 8 December, and on the night of Friday 11 December the dyke will come through at the Port. She asks for an order to raise the gate by day, on the afternoon ebb of Friday 11 December, with the meadows cleared.*

It was a letter over the mayor's head, from an employee whose resignation he kept in a folder with no date on it. Lesca knew what she was spending, and whose it was.

At the last line Maylis's right hand came up onto the table and took the pen. Maylis could have put a stroke through the sheet. She crossed a t that Lesca had left bare, low, and laid the pen down.

Lesca signed the letter *M. Lesca, keyholder*, and made the L as tall as the left hand could.

At first light she walked it up to the square. She held it in the slot of the yellow box and let it go. It dropped on top of Coralie's envelope.
