'You said you were coming to stay,' Maylis said. 'I've gone over what that is. It's a life in one hand. You'd have my legs, and my mouth, and my bed, and her in it. I'd have the right hand. I'd wash the cups with it while you told her what I wanted for supper. I'd sign where you put the pen. At night, when you were asleep, I could shut it.'

Lesca had a minute for forty houses. She had none for this, and gave none.

'The handover was for a night,' Maylis said. 'One. The gate, the winch and the telephone, and then you go home to your stone. I put *paid* at the foot of it. You don't get my house for a night's work you've done before. And you didn't ask for the house. You went up my stairs.'
