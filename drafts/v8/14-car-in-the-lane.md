'I saw your car on the first,' Maylis said. 'At dusk, in the lane behind the Castagnet house, without its lamps. He had gone down to his tonne.'

The wipers went across.

'And whose word is that?' Guy Larrère said. 'It's the word of the woman who carried chrysanthemums into that kitchen on a Sunday and sat twenty minutes, while the husband was at the café. The café has it on a pad, under a heading. Tell them my car, and they'll ask which of the Castagnets you were watching the house for.'

He untied the folder. A sheet on the prefecture's paper lay over her own.

'Pau sent me this on Wednesday, for my observations. A keyholder has written to the prefect over her mayor's head, asking for his gate.'
