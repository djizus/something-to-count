Monsieur Darrigrand had come up from the plots with his broom.

'Madame le maire. When you counted my house it came to four, against thirty-six, and you had it knocked down. This morning you counted Jean Castagnet, and he came to one, against forty.' He took the broom down. 'Your counting comes out right for you each time. I wanted that said to your face, with no minutes taken.'

He swept the tarmac where she had been standing, and went back down to his step.
