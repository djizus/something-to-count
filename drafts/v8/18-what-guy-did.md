'And there's one that wasn't in your speech,' Maïté said. 'Guy Larrère was in this kitchen that night. He came up the back lane when Jean's van went down. So he knew Jean was out. He rang you his order from that telephone. He sent nobody to the tonne, and he went home by the lane. For twelve years he's sat across a table from you and let you carry it.'

'His order was to keep it shut,' she said. 'Shut would have left your husband dry. I opened it. And it wasn't Guy who rang Jean's telephone once and put it away. Don't give me him for that. He has his own to carry.'

'I wasn't giving,' Maïté said. 'I was telling you whose kitchen it was.'
