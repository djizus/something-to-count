# Outline

Twenty-five chapters, 54,950 words. Odd chapters are Maylis and even ones Lesca. Chapter 1 is `manuscript/01.md` and is canon. G1 to G6, the leaks and the rungs use the bible's numbers. A glimpse holds only what the other has already lived. Chapters 1 to 15 are merged, and `briefs/figure-retrofit.md` lists what changes in them.

## Rations for 16 to 25

- A chapter carries one vote count and two sums of money at most, and 21 to 25 carry none.
- "I'll be brief" returns once, in 20. "Monsieur le Maire" returns once, in 22. "Still in the box" does not return, and 25 replaces it.
- Finger-counting is shown once a chapter at most, and a notebook entry is quoted for three lines at most.
- Each chapter has a Figure line. It follows the rungs in section 5 of the bible and its rule on what may repeat.

## Against JC's list, for 19 to 25

- **Stakes.** Each woman's choice costs a named person, with Coralie behind the bank on both nights: Jeannot in 19, Madame Etcheto in 23, and the grave in 25.
- **Humour.** Sarthou's invoice for fifty-four sandbags, Larrère cut off by his own timer, Madame Etcheto giving Inguma the sandbags to count, Hontaube's refusal before 1 January, and a numbered letter that is not a complaint.
- **Interesting characters.** Coralie in 20 and 23, Maïté in 24, and Jeannot in 25, who each do one thing the reader has not seen from them.
- **Plot and progression.** The river rises through 19 and 21, both refuse the chair in 22 and 23, and every chapter from 17 is shorter than the last.
- **Intrigues.** Why 02:51 and the unnamed Larrère in 19, what Coralie has kept in 20, the log and the kitchen in 24, the suitcase and letter 42 in 25. Which reason decided Lesca is never paid.
