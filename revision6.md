**Maylis, from inside.**
- 8h, at "Have Friday. I'll have yours", 60. She prices what she is offering: a night's work at forty euros the month, a van on a bank, and a woman thirty metres from the patch whom she leaves out of the sum. What she wants is a room that stands up when she comes in, and a thing asked for that is done by evening. It will not price, and she says it.

In front is the reflector. That morning she let it ring nine times, and he answered. She cannot be the one who did not come. She knows who is under the bank, and she goes on.
